About RemontCo

Connecting consumers with trusted, verified construction companies

Our Mission

RemontCo was created to solve a critical problem in the construction and renovation industry: protecting consumers from unregistered contractors and ensuring legal, transparent, and high-quality services.

We believe that every home improvement project should be performed by legitimate, registered companies that provide contracts, invoices, and full accountability.

Why Only Registered Companies?

Legal Protection

Official contracts and invoices for every job ensure legal recourse if something goes wrong.

Tax Compliance

All work is declared and taxed properly, supporting the economy and social systems.

Quality Assurance

Registered companies have reputations to maintain and can be held accountable.

Worker Protection

Legal entities ensure proper employment, insurance, and working conditions.

Verified Credentials

We verify every company's BULSTAT/EIK and legal registration status.

Fair Competition

Level playing field for legitimate businesses that pay taxes and follow regulations.

How We Protect Consumers

Verification Process

Every company must submit valid BULSTAT/EIK documentation and business registration proof.

Rating System

Transparent reviews from verified consumers help you make informed decisions.

Dispute Resolution

Admin moderation and clear processes for handling disputes and complaints.
